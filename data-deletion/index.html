Autom8 Integrations: Data Deletion Instructions

Autom8 Integrations (Meta App ID 3688512777983352) is a private advertising tool. It keeps no copy of your Facebook or Instagram profile data. It stores only an access token and a request log on the computer of the person who connected it. See the privacy policy.

Remove the app's access

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Find Autom8 Integrations and choose Remove.

The app can no longer access your data once it is removed.

Ask for stored data to be deleted

Email faisalahmed7170@gmail.com with the subject "Autom8 data deletion". Include your name and the Meta account or ad account involved. Within 30 days, any access token linked to you will be deleted, request-log entries about you will be deleted, and you'll get an email confirming it.

Ads, campaigns and reports inside Meta ad accounts are controlled in Meta Ads Manager by each account's owner, not by this app.
